## What was downloaded and checked

Everything was downloaded with `curl` into a fresh directory in the session scratchpad, outside the repository. Text was extracted with `pdftotext` or a tag-stripping script, and Python was run with `-I`. No earlier Stage 2 fix logs, oscillation reports or FROZEN_ITEMS.md exist, so no item is frozen.

- **[S-105] PDF** (the Joint Commission URL of section 10; HTTP 200).
  - Pages 6, 8 and 9 were extracted.
  - The developers sentence is on the page headed "PHQ and GAD-7 Instructions P.8/9", under "QUESTIONS REGARDING DEVELOPMENT, ACKNOWLEDGMENTS AND USE".
  - Page "P.9/9" is "SELECTED REFERENCES".
  - The PHQ-9 and GAD-7 ranges are on P.6/9, as cited.
- **[S-028] NVN module "Profylactische behandeling migraine"** (Stage 1 URL; HTTP 200; publication date 11-01-2018).
  - The Aanbeveling contains the Stage 1 OUT-018 quote verbatim.
  - The Aanbeveling begins with the advice, for prescribing prophylactics, to start low and increase slowly ("begin laag en verhoog traag"), followed by "Titreer naar de ondergrens van de geadviseerde therapeutische breedte".
- **[S-033] Russian clinical recommendations «Мигрень»** (Stage 1 URL; official PDF, 92 pages).
  - The Stage 1 quote "Рекомендуется считать профилактическое лечение М эффективным, если число дней с ГБ сокращается через 3 месяца терапии на 50% и более от исходного" is verbatim in section 7.1 "Оценка эффекта проводимой терапии" (p. 53). This is a general recommendation on preventive treatment of migraine.
  - A similar recommendation in the treatment section (PDF p. 38) also puts the assessment at 3 months.
- **[S-001] ICHD-3 PDF** (Stage 1 URL).
  - The 8.2 Description on p. 122 matches the quote in E-032 verbatim. It is reused in the new ISS-09.
  - Criterion B of 8.2 refers to "drugs that can be taken for acute and/or symptomatic treatment of headache", without any per-type restriction.
- **Instrument sources for R01-10.** The Europe PMC full-text XML of [S-014] (PMC6734476), [S-101] (PMC3057423) and [S-102] (PMC4406925), and the [S-103] page.
  - All value-set fragments used are verbatim: "four grades based on total days of impaired functioning" [S-102]; "the unit of each being whole days per one or 3 months" [S-014]; the HIT-6 category bands [S-101]; the PedMIDAS grading table [S-103].
  - The two sentences that the review proposes for whole-number scores describe how a total is obtained from the answers. They are [S-101] "A total score is calculated by adding the five headache-related disability items together." and [S-103] "The score is a simple composite of the total of six questions."
  - The "Scoring HALT" paragraph of [S-014] says that the HALT-90 total summed score also adds a simple count (question 5). That total is therefore still a whole number.
- **Stage 1 v18.** 23 Stage 1 fragments that v01 newly cites (for example "evalueer het effect niet eerder dan na 2-3 maanden behandeling", "через 3 месяца терапии", and the OUT-018 and OUT-023 Caveat sentences) were checked by script as exact substrings of Stage 1 v18.
