## Other changes

**Satisfiability pass.** Every MUST and SHOULD invariant and every presence constraint of a SHOULD attribute, or of a MUST attribute with a `when` clause, was checked against the definitional invariants and against each other for the elements and views they constrain, under INV-001. The pass found the following further conflicts, all of the R02-01/R02-02 kind: in some admissible context, the constraints cannot all hold together.

1. **INV-001: no course event precedes the start of its treatment.**
   - E-055 shows both `startDate` and `stopDate` (each SHOULD), and INV-079 (definitional) requires `s ≤ e`.
   - In v01, INV-001 admitted a stop event dated before the start, which made these constraints unsatisfiable.
   - The same ordering is step 2 of the R02-01 fix.
   - INV-001 now realises OUT-024 ("start and stop dates"), so the OUT-024 row lists INV-001.
2. **E-055 `startDate`: SHOULD only `when startOf(ctx, self.treatment) ≠ ∅`.**
   - INV-077 lists every treatment, including a preventive treatment without a start.
   - INV-079 requires a shown start date to be the start event, so the unconditional SHOULD could not be met.
3. **E-055 `preventiveResponse`: SHOULD only `when self.role = preventive ∧ startOf(ctx, self.treatment) ≠ ∅`.**
   - INV-056 (MUST) requires every response comparison to have `treatmentStart ∈ startOf(ctx, treatment)`.
   - The v01 SHOULD therefore required, for an unstarted preventive treatment, a comparison that the MUST forbids.
4. **E-055 `adherence`: the same condition.**
   - Adherence refers to the time on treatment (INV-067) and, by OUT-039, to "the evaluation window" of the response comparison. Neither exists without a start.
   - This follows R02-07: the v01 attribute required content that a correct output cannot contain.
5. **E-049 `onTreatmentPeriod`: SHOULD only `when ∃ s ∈ startOf(ctx, self.treatment) • s < self.eventDate`.**
   - INV-070 requires the period to begin on or after the start and to end before the event.
   - That is impossible for a stop, pause or reduction on the start day, which INV-001 admits.
   - The E-049 Conformance and Notes and the OUT-022 row are updated.
6. **E-058 `baselineResult`: SHOULD only when an earlier result of the same instrument is available.**
   - INV-082 (definitional) makes the baseline an earlier result with an available completion date (SHOULD).
   - For a first administration, the unconditional SHOULD could therefore not be met.
   - INV-081 already had this condition. The E-058 Conformance and Notes are updated.
7. **INV-062 and E-046 `otherValues`: the other values are required only where they are available.**
   - The new predicate `valueAvailable` (E-045) is true when a result of the instrument is available, or, for acute-treatment effectiveness, when the subject uses acute medication.
   - In v01, a label under, for example, IHS Q6 required MIDAS, HIT-6 and PGIC results even when none was available. Such a result cannot carry a completion date (E-058 SHOULD, INV-082).
   - The new condition is the model's own reference rule (section 3.5): the referenced requirements keep their conditions, OUT-026 "Where results of validated instruments are available" and OUT-023 "For each acute medication".
   - The E-045 Meaning, the E-046 Conformance and the OUT-020 row are updated.
8. **INV-087: the relations of cycle lines and attack marks now hold in every view (the R02-05 pattern).**
   - `firstDayOfMenstruation`, `perimenstrualWindow` (derived MUST) and `inPerimenstrualWindow` (derived MUST) had relations only for cycles of a menstrual-relation view.
   - INV-105 (already in v01) requires a cycle line in the patient-facing overview, where these derived values were therefore unconstrained.
   - The per-cycle relations now hold for every cycle line, with the view being `viewOf(cl)`. The in-window relation now holds for every attack mark, over the cycle lines of its view. A cycle's attack marks must be items of the same view.
   - The sequence structure, the overlap with the covered period and `completeCycles` remain properties of the menstrual-relation view.
   - For a menstrual-relation view whose cycle lines are its cycles, the meaning is unchanged.
   - INV-087 now realises OUT-035, so the OUT-035 row lists it. E-060, E-061 and E-067 Notes are updated.
9. **INV-105: treatment effect only where it can exist.**
   - The v01 antecedent `preventiveTreatments ≠ ∅ ∨ acuteMedications ≠ ∅` required a response comparison or an acute-treatment summary.
   - That cannot be met correctly for a subject whose only preventive treatments have not started (INV-056 forbids the comparison) and who uses no acute medication.
   - The antecedent is now "a preventive treatment has started, or the subject uses acute medication".
10. **E-062 `acute`: SHOULD only `when ctx.subject.acuteMedications ≠ ∅`.**
    - OUT-031 asks for effectiveness "for acute treatments", and INV-088 already quantifies over the acute medications. The E-062 Conformance is updated.
11. **INV-075: each associated-symptom status is shown once on a line.**
    - v01 admitted a line that shows the same symptom as both present and absent.
    - This is stated in the same way as the existing "one result per measure" conjunct of INV-075.
    - It was found while fixing R02-06 on the same attribute. INV-075 now realises OUT-012, so the OUT-012 row lists it.
12. **ISS-06: the consequence for the change from baseline.** "Effect on the model" now also notes that, for HURT, the change from baseline has no defined value, because INV-082 relates it to the total scores that HURT does not have. This is the R02-05 pattern, and its cause is the Stage 1 issue that ISS-06 already describes.
13. **Text.**
    - The INV-076 heading now reads "Associated symptoms in the response of treated attacks, for migraine", to match its condition; the ID is unchanged.
    - The traceability rows for OUT-010, OUT-012, OUT-018, OUT-020, OUT-021, OUT-022, OUT-024, OUT-027, OUT-030, OUT-034, OUT-035 and OUT-039 are updated.
    - Section 9 now reads "as of v02". Section 11 has a paragraph for iteration 02. Section 12 has the v02 entry.
14. **Considered and not changed.**
    - **E-062 `remissions` (SHOULD, a non-empty sequence).** Whether a remission period exists depends on diary information, not on a property of the context, so no condition can be stated without derivation from input. The attribute follows the wording of OUT-031. A view without remission periods, when none exists, is a deviation for a reason in particular circumstances, which SHOULD allows; it is not a conflict between two model constraints.
    - **INV-060 with INV-061 for percentage changes over periods shorter than 28 days.**
      - No invariant requires such a change: response comparisons are monthly (INV-056), and the scheme windows of the OUT-025 examples are at least 4 weeks.
      - A residual "per month" (OUT-019) within a shorter compared period does not exist by the wording of Stage 1 itself.
      - The design of INV-061 was set in iteration 01 (R01-03) and reviewed.
    - **E-039 `reviewWindow`** (SHOULD unconditionally) and the conditional conjunct of INV-050 are consistent: INV-051 defines the window for every withdrawal start.
    - **Derived attributes outside the R02-05 pattern.**
      - E-063 `duration` relates to the start and the end (or the covered period's end while ongoing). Both are SHOULD under the same conditions, so the value is defined in every fully conformant output.
      - The same holds for E-058 `category` and `changeFromBaseline`, except for HURT (item 12).
      - The status of a combined-use indicator (INV-046) is decided by the shown class indicators. INV-044 and INV-104 require those indicators wherever the combined indicator is required.
      - All other derived attributes are either fully related (E-005, E-032 `code` and `referenceLevel`, E-039, E-040, E-042, E-052 `proportion`, E-060 `completeCycles`) or values obtained from diary information.
