### CA-01 CONTRADICTION: INV-060 and INV-061 cannot both hold for a percentage change whose compared period is shorter than a month [impact: medium]
- Location: INV-060 (l. 2797–2808); INV-061 (l. 2810–2821); E-040 Conformance (l. 1075) and Notes (l. 1078); E-044 Notes (l. 1277); row OUT-019 (l. 3716).
- Conflicting texts:
  - INV-060 (SHOULD): `∀ ch : Change • ch.reference.measure ∈ {migraineDays, headacheDays} ∧ (∃ r ∈ ch.relative • r is percent) ⇒ ∃ rb ∈ viewOf(ch).content ∩ ResidualBurden • rb.change = ch ∧ …`
  - INV-061 (definitional): `∀ m ∈ {rb.migraineDays, rb.moderateSevereHeadacheDays} • m.period.kind ∈ MonthlyKind ∧ within(m.period, rb.change.compared.period) ∧ …`
  - E-040 Conformance: "A change shown outside a response comparison is permitted, in either form or in both; next to it, where it is a percentage change in migraine or headache days, the residual burden SHOULD be shown (INV-060, OUT-019), as next to every such change."
  - Row OUT-019: "full — residual migraine days and moderate/severe headache days per month next to every percentage change in migraine or headache days, also one shown in its percentage form only".
- Why they conflict: a calendar month or 28-day period has at least 28 days, so `within(m.period, compared.period)` is impossible when the compared period has fewer than 28 days (`checks_misc.py`: 9,855 compared spans of 1–27 days, 0 with a monthly period inside; e.g. the calendar week 2–8 March 2026). The model admits such a change (E-040 places no condition on the period kind; INV-053 only requires the same measure and scope and different dates; E-040 Notes name changes in the post-stop monitoring view, whose periods may have any length, and weekly series exist, OUT-009). For a percentage change in headache or migraine days between two calendar weeks, INV-060 asks for a residual burden that INV-061 (definitional, so at every level) forbids. INV-060 is therefore unmeetable for a class of outputs, while E-040 and row OUT-019 state it holds "next to every such change" with "full" coverage.
- Likely intended reading: Stage 1 OUT-019 ("Wherever the output shows a relative (percentage) change in migraine or headache days, it SHOULD show next to it the absolute number … per month that remain on treatment") and the prose: the residual burden is asked for next to every percentage change. INV-061's restriction of the residual month to a period *within* the compared period is the over-narrow side (it was written for monthly response comparisons, "as in every response comparison"). Either INV-061 must say which monthly period the residual of a sub-monthly change refers to, or the model must state, and record in section 8, that a per-month residual cannot accompany such a change.

### CA-02 CONTRADICTION: a definitional invariant (INV-031) makes the MAY element MigraineFreePeriodMark more required, which section 3.3 (5) says definitional invariants cannot do [impact: medium]
- Location: section 3.3 item 5 (l. 184); INV-031 last conjunct, Reading and Conformance (l. 2381–2385); E-022 Conformance and Notes (l. 726, 729); INV-104 (l. 3608–3611) and `summaryTimeCourse` (l. 1910–1911).
- Conflicting texts:
  - Section 3.3 (5): "A definitional invariant has no level of its own. … It therefore cannot make an element more or less required; it excludes only outputs that show a value contradicting the definition of the element".
  - INV-031, Conformance "definitional (E-017, E-018, E-020, E-021, E-022)": `((∃ mk ∈ v.content ∩ MigraineFreePeriodMark • mk.typeScope = sc) ∧ 0 ∈ daysOf(v, migraineDays, p, sc) ⇒ ∃ mk ∈ v.content ∩ MigraineFreePeriodMark • sameSpan(mk.period, spanOf(p)) ∧ mk.typeScope = sc)`; Reading: "a view that marks such periods marks every period whose migraine-day count is 0".
  - E-022 Conformance: "MAY; … In the clinician-facing summary SHOULD, for the covered period and each period of its series that shows 0 migraine days (INV-104 …)".
- Why they conflict: the conjunct is an existential presence requirement, not a relation between shown values. Reviews 21 and 22 examined the conjunct only for fidelity to Stage 1 ("keeps the marking of one view consistent"), not against section 3.3 (5).
- Likely intended reading: section 3.3 (5), which is the model's stated rule and the ground on which fixes 30 and 31 were decided. The conjunct is content ("periods without migraine days" marked consistently) and belongs at the level of OUT-008 (MAY) or of INV-104 (SHOULD), not in a definitional invariant; only the preceding conjunct (a mark implies a count of 0) is definitional.
